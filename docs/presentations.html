Posting your video

  1. Upload your video to Panopto or YouTube. Check that your classmates can watch it: on YouTube, "Unlisted" works; on Panopto, let anyone at the University of Chicago with the link view it.
  2. Open the presentations list on GitHub and sign in. If GitHub asks, let it make a copy (a "fork") of the repository for you.
  3. Find your name. Paste your video's link between the quotes on the video: line right under it, like this: 
        - name: Your Name
      library: your library
      video: "https://youtu.be/your-video"
    Change nothing else.
  4. Click Commit changes… (or Propose changes), then Create pull request.

Chelsea will merge it, and your link will show up here a few minutes later.

Bonus points are added to your presentation score, and they aren't capped at full credit: a 30/30 plus 3 bonus points keeps all 3 toward your final grade.
